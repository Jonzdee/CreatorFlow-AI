# CreatorFlow AI Vision

## Mission

Empower creators worldwide with an AI assistant that understands every major social media platform, helps generate engaging content, recommends the best times to post, and enables creators to grow confidently without relying on a full-time social media manager.

## Core Principles

- Mobile First
- AI First
- Creator First
- Simplicity
- Consistency
- Data-Driven Recommendations

## Our Promise

CreatorFlow AI doesn't replace creativity.

It removes the stress of planning, writing, scheduling, and optimizing content so creators can focus on creating.
